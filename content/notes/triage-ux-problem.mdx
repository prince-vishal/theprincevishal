<Lede>
  After six months of watching analysts work, I am convinced that what SOC
  teams need is less AI and more reorganization.
</Lede>

The good AI helps with that too, but only if it starts from the workflow:
what gets grouped, what gets hidden, what needs evidence, and where a human
should be asked before the system gets clever.
