§ chapter three

things I've come to believe about building.

i.

Demos are easy. Tuesdays are hard. Build for Tuesday.

ii.

The interesting AI problem is almost never the model. It's the boundary around the model.

iii.

Approvals, audit logs, and undo aren't UX features — they're what makes a thing usable in the real world.

iv.

If your platform requires a Slack message to ship code, your platform isn't done.

v.

Threat intel is mostly a data-modelling problem wearing a cybersecurity hat.

vi.

You can't outsource taste. The smallest details are the ones nobody else will fix.

vii.

Object storage is doing more than people give it credit for.

viii.

The best engineering leaders I know spend more time removing things than adding them.
